# Pony Driland — AI Usage Policy

Welcome to the official AI Usage Policy for **Pony Driland**.

This document explains, in plain language, how Artificial Intelligence (AI) may be
used in our project, and what artists, writers, and contributors can expect when
they join us.

We wrote this to be clear. If you have any doubt after reading it, please ask
before you contribute. It is always better to ask than to guess.

## 1. Our Core Principle

Pony Driland is a community project. We value human creativity above everything
else, but we also accept that AI is a tool. Like any tool, it depends on who uses
it and how.

Our policy has one single goal: **protect the original artistic identity of the
artist, while allowing the project to grow with the help of technology.**

---

## 2. AI Usage in Art

This section applies to **all art forms**, including but not limited to:

- Drawings and illustrations
- Animations
- Voice acting
- Music
- Any other creative medium

### 2.1 What You Agree To

By contributing art to Pony Driland, you agree that anything you create for the
project **may eventually be used in AI systems** for any project purpose.

### 2.2 What We Protect

Our protection is limited to **one thing only**: your **original art style**.

To make this clear, here is exactly what the AI does and does not do with your
artwork.

> **Exception:** This section only covers content with a visual identity. Voice
> acting, music, sound effects, and other non-visual works are not covered here.
> See [Section 2.4](#24-audio-and-non-visual-content-exception).

**The AI is used to:**

- Isolate and extract the **original elements of Pony Driland** (characters,
  world, symbols, and visual identity) from the artwork.
- Adapt the artwork for the sole purpose of **keeping only the original Pony
  Driland elements** in the final result.

**The AI is not used to:**

- Extract, learn, copy, or reproduce your **original artistic traits**.
- Build a style model, a style replica, or a style reference from your work.
- Adapt your artwork in a way that preserves your personal style.

In other words: the AI is used to remove **what is not Pony Driland**. It is never
used to remove **what is yours**.

### 2.3 When This Rule Does Not Apply

If your art style is **not considered original** (for example, if it uses a very
common style already used by many other artists and industry projects in general),
then there is no original style to protect, and the protection described in
[Section 2.2](#22-what-we-protect) does not apply.

### 2.4 Audio and Non-Visual Content Exception

The protection described in [Section 2.2](#22-what-we-protect) only applies to
content that has a **visual identity**. If a work has no visual identity, there is
no visual style to protect, and this section applies instead.

**Content covered by this section:**

- Voice acting
- Music and songs
- Sound effects
- Any other work that cannot have a visual identity

**Recommendation for voice actors:**

Always perform with a **fictional voice** (a character voice), never with your
natural speaking voice.

**Recommendation for musicians and sound designers:**

Create material **for the project**, not personal signature work. Treat the audio
you send as a contribution to Pony Driland, not as a portfolio piece.

**Why this matters:**

- A fictional voice belongs to the character. If it is ever cloned, the clone
  copies the character, not you.
- Your natural voice stays out of the project material. It cannot be cloned from
  something that was never recorded.
- This protects your real identity, your real voice, and your future work.

**What this means in practice:**

- The project may use AI to process, adapt, remix, or clone the **audio** you
  performed or composed.
- The project will **not** ask you to record with your natural voice.
- If you choose to use your natural voice anyway, you do so knowing that it is
  **not** protected by Section 2.2.

## 3. AI Usage in Writing (Fan Fiction)

AI may be used in the writing of Pony Driland fan fiction, but only for the
following purposes:

- **Text correction** (grammar, spelling, and clarity).
- **Inspiration** to help the writer develop their own content.

### 3.1 Translations

AI-generated translations are allowed, but with one condition: the translation
**must be reviewed** to confirm that the text matches the personality and tone of
the original work.

## 4. Community Ownership

If you want to participate in Pony Driland, you must be aware of the following:

> Everything you develop for Pony Driland belongs **exclusively to the Pony
> Driland community**, and you **cannot revoke this right**.

This is not meant to be harsh. It exists so the project can keep existing and
growing for everyone in the long term.

---

## 5. Official Content and AI

If your content is marked as **official Pony Driland content**, you must be aware
that it **may eventually be used by community AI content**.

Our AI content restrictions are limited only to the rules that protect your
original artistic style, as explained in [Section 2](#2-ai-usage-in-art).

---

## 6. Who Can Join the Team

### 6.1 Human Artists specialized in AI

Human artists who use AI are **welcome**, as long as they can efficiently prove
that they have the technical ability to use this tool **together with** the
technical knowledge of an artist.

### 6.2 Organic Artists

Traditional (organic) artists are **welcome**, following the same criteria as
artists who use AI.

### 6.3 Prompters

Prompters **can** participate in Pony Driland. However, the probability of
acceptance is **very rare**, because most AI-related tasks can be more easily
managed by the original creator of Pony Driland.

---

## 7. Team Priority Order

When adding members to the official Pony Driland team, we follow this priority
order:

| Priority         | Role                                                    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **High**         | Human artist who knows how to manage AI                 |
| **Normal**       | Traditional human artist                                |
| **Very low**     | Prompter                                                |

---

## 8. Human-First Content Policy

The priority of the project is always to keep a balance that favors
**content created by humans**.

This means:

1. Any AI-generated content published officially is listed to be **replaced in
   the future** by a more human work, if a human has the ability to do a better
   job.
2. If a replacement happens, the artist **will receive credit**.
3. For transparency, we will also inform **which previous content** the artist
   used as a base to create their version.

---

## 9. Summary

- AI in art is allowed, but your **original style** is protected.
- AI in writing is only for **correction and inspiration**.
- AI translations are allowed **with review**.
- Everything you create for the project belongs to the **community**.
- **Humans come first**, always.

## 10. Scope and Enforcement

This policy is a contract between Pony Driland and the people who work on it. It
only has power where that contract exists.

### 10.1 Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies **only** to:

- Members of the official Pony Driland team.
- Contributors who have agreed to this policy.
- Content that is marked as **official Pony Driland content**.

### 10.2 Who This Policy Does Not Apply To

This policy does **not** apply to:

- Fan-made content.
- Unofficial projects.
- Any community member who is not part of the official project.

If you are not an official member, [Section 4](#4-community-ownership) does not
apply to you. Your work is not covered by it, and it is not taken by it.

### 10.3 Protection for Everyone Else

If you are not an official member of Pony Driland, your work is **yours**.

We will **not** use your work to train AI without your explicit permission. This
includes art, writing, music, voice, and any other material you publish.

Using the work of a community member for AI training without authorization is a
violation of this policy.

### 10.4 Consequences

A violation of this policy may result in official community punishment. This
includes, but is not limited to:

- Removal of the offending content.
- Removal from the official team.
- A ban from the community.

## 11. Credits and Transparency

Following the spirit of [Section 8](#8-human-first-content-policy), we state
clearly how this document was made.

- **Text drafting:** This page was written with the help of an AI language model.
- **Creative direction:** Every rule, exception, and priority in this document was
  defined by a human. The AI did not decide the policy. It only organized the
  text.
- **Review:** The final text was read, corrected, and approved by a human.

We are stating this for a practical reason. An AI model, when left alone, produces
generic results. This document only became specific because a human guided the
entire creative process: choosing what to protect, what to allow, and what to
refuse. The AI had no opinion about Pony Driland. The human did.

This is the same principle we apply to all AI content in this project.
